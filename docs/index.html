How you use it

Press, talk, Enter. That's it.

Press your hotkey

Any free key you like. You pick it once at setup.

Talk

An island at the bottom of your screen moves with your voice.

Press Enter

The text is pasted at your cursor. Esc cancels.

The island tells you what's happening

Two commands. Then just talk.

$ cd justyap && ./install.sh
